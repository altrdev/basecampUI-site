Under the hood

Simple on the surface.
Rigorous underneath.

Zero build

Vanilla, by choice.

Plain JavaScript and web components. No framework, no bundler, no waiting.

Zero wiring

Built from your home.

Rooms, devices and labels come straight from Home Assistant’s registries. Nothing is hard-wired by hand.

Safe

Read-only by default.

The interface can only call a short, explicit list of services. Sensitive ones demand a long press or a code.

Tablet first

Made for the wall.

Designed for 1280×800 wall tablets, with generous touch targets and a dark, glare-free palette.

Tested

Logic you can trust.

Billing, schedules, alerts and ventilation live in pure modules, covered by tests that need no browser.

Private

Local, always.

No cloud and no token in the code. It runs inside your own Home Assistant, on your own network.
