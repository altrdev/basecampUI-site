Basecamp UI

Your home.
At a glance.

A custom interface for Home Assistant. Designed for the tablet on your wall, and just as at home on your phone.

Everything that matters.
Nothing that doesn’t.

Weather, energy, rooms, security and access on one calm screen you can read from across the room. Big numbers, soft colour, zero clutter.

Rooms

Every room,
one tap away.

Each zone shows temperature, humidity, lights and open windows at a glance. Tap in for climate, dimmers and air quality, with controls made for real fingers.

Energy

Watch the sun do the work.

Live flows between solar, grid and home. Hour-by-hour power, self-sufficiency and tomorrow’s forecast. Your energy story in one picture.

Weather

Weather from your own backyard.

Readings from your station, the last 24 hours and the next 24 on a single line. Wind, rain, humidity and pressure, beautifully quiet.

Security

Safe by design.

Doors, windows, alarm and who’s home. Risky actions need a deliberate press and critical ones a code, so a stray touch never opens the gate.

Shutters & awnings

Light, exactly
how you like it.

Every shutter and awning in one place, with presets that take a single tap. Sand-coloured sliders that feel like the real thing.

Everywhere

Same home.
Pocket size.

The layout reflows for phones and foldables without losing a single control. Open it from the Home Assistant app and you are home.

Inside

Simple on the surface.
Rigorous underneath.

Zero build

Vanilla, by choice.

Plain JavaScript and web components. No framework, no bundler, no waiting.

Zero wiring

Built from your home.

Rooms, devices and labels come straight from Home Assistant’s registries. Nothing is hard-wired by hand.

Safe

Read-only by default.

The interface can only call a short, explicit list of services. Sensitive ones demand a long press or a code.

Tablet first

Made for the wall.

Designed for 1280×800 wall tablets, with generous touch targets and a dark, glare-free palette.

Tested

Logic you can trust.

Billing, schedules, alerts and ventilation live in pure modules, covered by tests that need no browser.

Private

Local, always.

No cloud and no token in the code. It runs inside your own Home Assistant, on your own network.
